Assumptions and limitations

This is an independently developed Riverside calculator modeled on the general comparison approach of the referenced CodePen, not a copy of its source. It assumes a fixed monthly promotional payment, nominal APR divided by 12, no financed origination fees, no prepayment penalties, no solar maintenance or replacement costs, and full availability of estimated savings. Refinancing is not guaranteed. The 14% reset is modeled as a rate change with the same payment unless changed by contract; real Financeit terms may differ. A payment that does not cover interest creates negative amortization. GST/tax treatment and the merchant fee base must be verified for each transaction. This tool does not model the tax implications of home financing.
