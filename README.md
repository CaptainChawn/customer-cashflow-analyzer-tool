# Riverside Solar Cash Flow Analyzer v4

Static client-facing calculator. Deploy `index.html` at repository root through Cloudflare Pages (no build step).

## Changes
- Cash-purchase simple payback year and vertical payback marker.
- Cash-purchase cumulative cash flow including rebate and electricity escalation.
- Solar cash net benefit versus a hypothetical taxable investment's after-tax gain at the chosen horizon.
- NPV of solar cashflows using selected investment return as the discount rate.
- Retains Financeit promotional expiry and mortgage/HELOC refinancing balance charts.

## Important
The investment comparison assumes the original project price is invested, with all gains taxed on liquidation at the selected horizon. It does not reinvest solar savings and does not model capital-gains basis changes, registered accounts, inflation-adjusted values, or system replacement/maintenance costs. Rebate eligibility and timing must be verified for each customer. Electricity savings are simplified blended estimates and are not guaranteed. Financing contract terms must be checked.
